## cue 01
Finally, the settings screen. Open it from the gear button in the toolbar.

## cue 02
"Auto-advance after decision" is on by default. Turn it off to stay on the current reference after deciding and move at your own pace.

## cue 03
"Show progress at bottom" switches where progress is displayed. Turned off, progress appears prominently above the title.

## cue 04
Team-related options such as assignment sets are also managed here. Adjust everything to fit your working style.

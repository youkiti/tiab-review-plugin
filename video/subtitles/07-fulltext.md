## cue 01
References included at the title/abstract stage move on to full-text screening in the "Full-text" tab. Buttons at the top switch between three views: candidate list, AI screening, and post-decision review.

## cue 02
The candidate list shows the PDF retrieval status of each reference. By default, references you included at TiAb become candidates, but a multi-reviewer consensus rule can also be configured.

## cue 03
The "Fetch free full texts" button searches open-access routes such as PMC and Unpaywall in order, and saves retrieved PDFs to the project's Drive folder automatically. You can also upload your own PDFs.

## cue 04
Clicking a candidate card opens the full-text page, with the PDF and a decision panel side by side.

## cue 05
Read the text and judge with Include, Exclude, or Maybe. The same keyboard shortcuts as the Manual tab work here too.

## cue 06
Choosing Exclude shows a reason picker — Population, Intervention, and so on. Selecting a reason saves the decision and advances to the next candidate.

## cue 07
The "Post-decision review" view aggregates results per reviewer and computes PRISMA full-text-stage numbers automatically. CSV/RIS export and manuscript text generation are also available here.

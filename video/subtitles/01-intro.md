## cue 01
TiAb Review Plugin is a Chrome extension that streamlines study screening for systematic reviews.

## cue 02
It uses a Google Spreadsheet as a shared database, so multiple reviewers can work on the same project.

## cue 03
This video walks through the whole workflow: signing in, title/abstract screening, machine-learning and AI assistance, full-text screening, and team sharing.

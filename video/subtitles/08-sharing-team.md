## cue 01
To review as a team, add collaborators from the toolbar's "Share" button. Invite them by their Google account email address.

## cue 02
If the project has a Drive folder, the whole folder is shared, giving edit access to the spreadsheet and full-text PDFs together. A "Copy invite message" feature prepares a ready-to-send guide including setup instructions.

## cue 03
Access can be revoked from the ✕ button on each user's row.

## cue 04
While the "Blind" switch is on, other reviewers' decisions are hidden. After independent dual screening, switch it off to reveal conflicts and start resolving them.

## cue 05
The team progress panel shows every member's decision count and last activity. To preserve blinding, the include/exclude breakdown is never shown.

## cue 01
To review as a team, add collaborators from the toolbar's "Share" button. Invite them by their Google account email address.

## cue 02
If the project has a Drive folder, the whole folder is shared, giving edit access to the spreadsheet and full-text PDFs together. A "Copy invite message" feature prepares a ready-to-send guide including setup instructions.

## cue 03
Access can be revoked from the ✕ button on each user's row.

## cue 04
The switch that shows others' decisions is off by default, so other reviewers' decisions stay hidden. After independent dual screening, an admin turns the switch on to open the key, reveal conflicts, and start resolving them. The change applies to the whole project.

## cue 05
The team progress panel shows every member's decision count and last activity. To keep decisions independent, the include/exclude breakdown is never shown.

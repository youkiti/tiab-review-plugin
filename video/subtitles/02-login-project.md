## cue 01
When you install the extension and open the side panel, the login screen appears first.

## cue 02
Click "Sign in with Google" to authenticate. On first login you will be asked to grant access to Google APIs. Once granted, the extension reconnects automatically from then on.

## cue 03
After signing in, you reach the project selection screen. A project is simply a spreadsheet in your Google Drive.

## cue 04
To start a new review, use the "Create New Review Project" button. A spreadsheet with the required sheet structure is created in your Drive automatically.

## cue 05
To connect to an existing project, enter a spreadsheet URL or ID, or pick one from the list of recent spreadsheets.

## cue 06
Click "Connect to selected sheet" to load the references and move to the screening view.

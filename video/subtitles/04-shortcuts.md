## cue 01
Keyboard shortcuts speed up screening: press I for Include, E for Exclude, and M for Maybe.

## cue 02
Use the left and right arrow keys to move between references.

## cue 03
Reading, deciding, and advancing can all be done from the keyboard, so large sets of references can be processed efficiently. Shortcuts are disabled while you are typing in a note or search field.

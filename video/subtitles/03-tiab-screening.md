## cue 01
This is the title/abstract screening view, the "Manual" tab. You review each reference's title and abstract and make a decision one by one.

## cue 02
Import references from the toolbar's Import button. Major formats are supported, including RIS, PubMed NBIB, and ClinicalTrials.gov CSV.

## cue 03
Decisions are made with the three buttons: Include, Maybe, and Exclude. After each decision the view advances automatically, and the result is saved to the spreadsheet.

## cue 04
When you choose Exclude, you can record the reason for exclusion.

## cue 05
With keyword highlighting, inclusion-related keywords appear in green and exclusion-related keywords in red. Keywords are freely editable and shared with the whole team.

## cue 06
You can leave notes on anything worth remembering. Notes are saved automatically.

## cue 07
Status filters let you narrow the list, for example to pending references only, or to those you included.

## cue 08
Your own decision count and overall progress are always visible. When everything is judged, a completion banner appears and guides you to the next step.
